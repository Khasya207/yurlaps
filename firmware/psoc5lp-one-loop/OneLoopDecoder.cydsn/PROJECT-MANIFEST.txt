PSoC Creator target: CY8C5888LTI-LP097 (CY8CKIT-059)
Master/BUS clock: 80 MHz from 5 MHz ECO through PLL x16

Required exact component/API names:
  LoopIn       Digital Input Pin, P12[2], rising interrupt
  HystOut      Hardware Digital Output Pin, P12[3], driven by LoopIn net
  LoopEdgeISR  Interrupt connected to LoopIn interrupt
  SampleClock  20 MHz Clock connected to SampleDMA drq
  SampleDMA    DMA, rising-edge hardware request
  HostUART     UART, 57600 8-N-1
  HostRx       Digital Input Pin, P12[6], connected to HostUART rx
  HostTx       Digital Output Pin, P12[7], connected to HostUART tx
  StatusLED    Software Digital Output Pin, P2[1], active low

Source files to add to project:
  main.c
  capture_dma.c
  passage_tracker.c
  rch_demod.c
  rch_protocol.c
  serial_console.c
  timebase.c
